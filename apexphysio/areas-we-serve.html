Why a Clinic Close to Home Helps

Recovery usually takes more than one visit. A clinic a few minutes from home or work makes it easier to keep your appointments and stay on track with your plan.

We direct bill most insurers, including WCB, motor vehicle (MVA) claims and many extended health plans. In Alberta you can see a physiotherapist without a doctor's referral.
