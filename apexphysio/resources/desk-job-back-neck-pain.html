Desk Job Back and Neck Pain: 5 Simple Habits That Help

Small changes to how you sit and move during the day can make a real difference.

📅 Book Online 📞 825-509-7141

1. Raise your screen

Set the top of your screen at about eye level, an arm's length away, so you are not looking down for hours.

2. Support your feet and back

Sit with your feet flat on the floor or a footrest and your lower back supported by the chair.

3. Move every 30 to 45 minutes

Stand up, walk to refill your water, or take a call on your feet. The best posture is the next one.

4. Add two short stretches

Gentle chin tucks and shoulder rolls a few times a day can ease stiffness. Stop if anything causes sharp pain.

5. Know when to get it checked

See a physiotherapist if pain lasts more than a couple of weeks, keeps coming back, or spreads into an arm or leg. Not sure where your pain is coming from? Try our Click Where It Hurts 3D map.

This article is general information, not medical advice. If you have severe or worsening symptoms, see a doctor or go to emergency.
