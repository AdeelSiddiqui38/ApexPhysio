Hurt in a Car Accident in Calgary? What to Do in the First Weeks

Practical first steps after a collision, and how physiotherapy and auto insurance billing work at our Nolan Hill clinic.

📅 Book Online 📞 825-509-7141

1. Get checked first

If you hit your head, feel numbness or tingling, have severe pain, or symptoms that get worse, see a doctor or go to emergency right away. Some symptoms, like neck stiffness from whiplash, can show up a day or two after the collision.

2. Report the collision to your insurer

Call your auto insurer as soon as you can and write down your claim number. You will need it for treatment and billing.

3. You can start physiotherapy without a referral

In Alberta you can book physiotherapy directly. Many common collision injuries, such as whiplash and other sprains and strains, are treated under Alberta's auto insurance rules, and we can direct bill your auto insurer. Bring your claim number and insurer details to your first visit, and ask us if you are unsure what is covered.

4. What your first visit looks like

  • A one-on-one assessment of your injury and how you are moving
  • A clear explanation of what is going on
  • A treatment plan and simple exercises to start at home

5. Keep moving, gently

For most sprain and strain injuries, staying gently active within comfort usually helps more than complete rest. Your physiotherapist will tell you what is safe for you.

This article is general information, not medical advice. If you have severe or worsening symptoms, see a doctor or go to emergency.
