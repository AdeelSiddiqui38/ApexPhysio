Injured at Work? How WCB Physiotherapy Works at Apex Physio

What to do after a workplace injury, and how we work with WCB-Alberta claims at our Nolan Hill clinic.

📅 Book Online 📞 825-509-7141

1. Report the injury

Tell your employer about the injury as soon as possible, and report it to WCB-Alberta. Keep your WCB claim number handy.

2. Get the right care early

If the injury is serious, see a doctor or go to emergency first. For muscle, joint and back injuries, starting physiotherapy early can help you stay active and plan a safe return to work.

3. Bring your claim details

Bring your WCB claim number and your employer's details to your first visit. We can bill WCB directly, so you are not paying out of pocket for approved treatment.

4. What treatment focuses on

  • An assessment of your injury and the demands of your job
  • Hands-on care and exercise to restore strength and movement
  • A step-by-step plan for returning to work safely

This article is general information, not medical advice. If you have severe or worsening symptoms, see a doctor or go to emergency.
