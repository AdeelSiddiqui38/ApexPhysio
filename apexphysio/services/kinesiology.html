FAQ

Common Questions

How is kinesiology different from physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy often leads with hands-on treatment for acute pain; kinesiology is exercise-focused, helping rebuild strength and function once you're ready to progress actively — the two work well together.

Is kinesiology covered by WCB or insurance?

Yes, kinesiology is commonly covered under WCB return-to-work programs and many extended health plans. We offer direct billing where available.

Do I need to already be doing physiotherapy?

No — kinesiology can be its own service, especially for return-to-sport training, general reconditioning or chronic pain management.

What should I wear to a session?

Comfortable athletic clothing and running shoes — sessions are active and may involve light equipment.
