ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Physiotherapy in Nolan Ridge, NW Calgary

Physiotherapy is a regulated health profession focused on how your body moves. At Apex Physio & Wellness Clinic, physiotherapy starts with a full assessment: your history, how the problem started, what makes it better or worse, and how you move, so treatment is aimed at the cause and not only the sore spot.

From there, your physiotherapist combines hands-on care (joint mobilisation, soft-tissue techniques), exercise you can actually do at home, and clear education about what is going on. Our clinic at Unit 150, 246 Nolanridge Crescent NW is a short drive from Nolan Hill, Sage Hill, Evanston, Kincora, Sherwood, Carrington and Livingston, with free parking at the door.

In Alberta you do not need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist. Some insurance plans ask for one, so check your plan before your first visit if you're unsure.

What a Physiotherapy Plan Can Include

  • Assessment of movement, strength, joints and posture
  • Manual therapy and soft-tissue techniques
  • A home exercise program, progressed as you improve
  • IMS or dry needling, where appropriate and with your consent
  • Taping, education and activity modification
  • Return-to-sport, return-to-work and post-surgical rehab plans

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No — in Alberta you can book physiotherapy directly. A referral may still be useful for certain insurance claims, so check with your provider if you're unsure.

Will you direct bill my insurance?

Yes, we offer direct billing to most major extended health providers, plus MVA and WCB claims, so you're not paying out of pocket and waiting on a reimbursement.

How long is a session?

Initial assessments and follow-up visits are full-length, one-on-one appointments — you're not sharing your clinician's attention with another patient in the same time slot.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Comfortable clothing you can move in, your insurance information if you have extended health coverage, and any relevant imaging or reports if applicable.

How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem, how long you've had it and your goals. After your assessment your physiotherapist will explain a suggested plan and review it with you as you go. You can stop or change the plan at any time.

Can physiotherapy help after surgery?

Yes, physiotherapy is a common part of recovery after orthopaedic surgery such as knee or shoulder procedures. Bring any instructions from your surgeon so your physiotherapist can follow their protocol.

What is the difference between physiotherapy and chiropractic care?

Both treat muscle and joint problems. Physiotherapy places more emphasis on exercise, rehab and movement retraining; chiropractic care places more emphasis on spinal and joint adjustment. At Apex the two can work together on one plan.

We're in Nolan Ridge, NW Calgary, and see people from Nolan Hill, Sage Hill, Evanston, Kincora, Sherwood, Carrington and Livingston and the rest of NW Calgary, with easy access from Stoney Trail.
