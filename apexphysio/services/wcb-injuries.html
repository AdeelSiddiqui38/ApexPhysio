FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a WCB claim number before booking?

It's helpful to have one, but we can help guide you through getting set up if your claim is still in progress.

Will you bill WCB directly?

Yes, for approved treatment we bill WCB Alberta directly, so there's no out-of-pocket cost for covered care.

Can you help with modified duties at work?

Yes — we can provide functional capacity input and work with you and your employer on modified-duty recommendations as part of your recovery plan.

What if my employer needs updates on my progress?

We provide the standard WCB progress reporting your employer and case manager need to track your return-to-work timeline.
