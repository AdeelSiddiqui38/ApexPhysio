ABOUT THIS SERVICE

WCB Workplace Injury Rehab in NW Calgary

If you're hurt at work in Alberta, report the injury to your employer and to WCB-Alberta. At Apex Physio & Wellness Clinic we focus on helping you recover and return to work safely, with treatment built around the demands of your job.

Your plan may combine physiotherapy, chiropractic care and kinesiology-led active rehab. With your consent, we keep your WCB case manager updated on progress and recommendations, including modified or graduated duties.

Our clinic at Unit 150, 246 Nolanridge Crescent NW serves workers from Nolan Hill, Sage Hill, Evanston, Kincora, Sherwood, Carrington and Livingston and across NW Calgary, with free parking at the door.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a WCB claim number before booking?

It's helpful to have one, but we can help guide you through getting set up if your claim is still in progress.

Will you bill WCB directly?

For accepted claims we aim to bill WCB-Alberta directly for approved treatment. We'll confirm the billing for your claim before you start.

Can you help with modified duties at work?

Yes — we can provide functional capacity input and work with you and your employer on modified-duty recommendations as part of your recovery plan.

What if my employer needs updates on my progress?

We provide the standard WCB progress reporting your employer and case manager need to track your return-to-work timeline.

What should I bring to my first WCB appointment?

Bring your WCB claim number if you have one, your employer's details and the date of injury. If your claim is still being set up, contact us and we'll explain the next steps.

How long does WCB treatment usually take?

It depends on the injury and the demands of your job. Your plan is reviewed regularly with you and, with your consent, your case manager.

Can I choose my own clinic for WCB treatment?

Talk with your WCB case manager about where you'd like to be treated; they can explain what applies to your claim.

What is a graduated return to work?

It's a plan where hours or duties increase step by step as you recover. We can share recommendations with your employer and case manager, with your consent.
