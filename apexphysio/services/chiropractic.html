HOW IT WORKS

What to Expect

A thorough, hands-on approach — never a rushed, five-minute adjustment.

1

Assessment & History

Your chiropractor examines your spine, posture and joint movement, and reviews your health history before any hands-on treatment.

2

Spinal & Joint Adjustment

Precise, targeted adjustments to restore movement to restricted joints — explained clearly before anything is done.

3

Supportive Techniques

Soft tissue work, mobilization or stretching added as needed to support the adjustment and speed recovery.

4

Ongoing Care Plan

A visit frequency and home-care plan matched to your goals, not a one-size-fits-all package.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is chiropractic adjustment safe?

Yes — it's a well-studied, widely used treatment. Your chiropractor will explain the plan and get your consent before every adjustment.

Do I need a referral to see a chiropractor?

No referral is needed — you can book directly. Check your insurance plan if you need one for reimbursement purposes.

Will you direct bill my insurance?

Yes, we offer direct billing to most major extended health providers, so there's no waiting on reimbursement.

How many visits will I need?

It varies by condition — some people feel relief after one visit, others benefit from a short structured plan. Your chiropractor will set expectations after your first assessment.
