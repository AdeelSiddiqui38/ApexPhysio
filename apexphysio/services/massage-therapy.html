Massage Therapy in Nolan Ridge, NW Calgary

Massage therapy at Apex Physio & Wellness Clinic is treatment-focused. Your massage therapist starts with a short conversation about what's bothering you, any injuries or health conditions, and your pressure preferences, then focuses the session on the muscles and tissues involved.

Because massage is offered alongside physiotherapy, chiropractic and kinesiology, your therapist can coordinate with the rest of your care team when you are being treated for an injury, so everyone works from the same plan.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a referral for massage therapy?

No referral is required to book. Some insurance plans do require one for reimbursement, so it's worth checking your policy.

Is massage therapy covered by insurance?

Most extended health plans include massage therapy coverage, and we offer direct billing to most major providers.

What's the difference between relaxation and therapeutic massage?

Relaxation massage uses lighter, even pressure for stress relief. Therapeutic and deep tissue work targets specific problem areas with firmer, more focused techniques.

How often should I get a massage?

It depends on your goals — weekly or biweekly for an active injury, monthly for general maintenance. Your therapist will suggest a schedule after your first visit.

What should I wear for a massage?

You'll undress to your comfort level and be covered with a sheet the whole time, with only the area being treated uncovered. Tell your therapist if you'd prefer a different approach.

Is massage safe during pregnancy?

Prenatal massage is commonly provided during pregnancy with adjusted positioning. Tell us you're pregnant when booking, and check with your doctor or midwife if you have a higher-risk pregnancy.

Will deep tissue massage hurt?

You may feel some discomfort in tight areas, but it should never be more than you're comfortable with. Tell your therapist at any time and they'll adjust the pressure.

Can I combine massage with physiotherapy?

Yes. Many patients book massage alongside physiotherapy or chiropractic care, and our team can share notes (with your consent) so treatment fits together.
