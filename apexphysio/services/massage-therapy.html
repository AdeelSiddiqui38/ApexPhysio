FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a referral for massage therapy?

No referral is required to book. Some insurance plans do require one for reimbursement, so it's worth checking your policy.

Is massage therapy covered by insurance?

Most extended health plans include massage therapy coverage, and we offer direct billing to most major providers.

What's the difference between relaxation and therapeutic massage?

Relaxation massage uses lighter, even pressure for stress relief. Therapeutic and deep tissue work targets specific problem areas with firmer, more focused techniques.

How often should I get a massage?

It depends on your goals — weekly or biweekly for an active injury, monthly for general maintenance. Your therapist will suggest a schedule after your first visit.
