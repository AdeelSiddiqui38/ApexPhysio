ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Acupuncture, IMS and Dry Needling in NW Calgary

Acupuncture, intramuscular stimulation (IMS) and dry needling all use fine, sterile, single-use needles, but for different purposes. IMS and dry needling target tight or sensitive muscles and trigger points; acupuncture is based on traditional point locations. At Apex Physio & Wellness Clinic, needling is offered by trained practitioners as one part of a broader treatment plan.

Before any needling, your practitioner reviews your health history, explains what's involved and what you might feel, and asks for your consent. Needling is usually combined with exercise and hands-on treatment, not used on its own.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does acupuncture or IMS hurt?

Most people feel little to no discomfort — sometimes a brief muscle twitch or dull ache at the point, which fades quickly. Needles are much finer than injection needles.

What's the difference between acupuncture and IMS?

Traditional acupuncture follows meridian-based points; IMS is a Western medical approach targeting specific tight muscle bands and trigger points related to nerve irritation.

Is it covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans include acupuncture coverage. We'll confirm your coverage and offer direct billing where available.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies from person to person and by condition. Your practitioner will discuss a plan after your first visit.

Is dry needling the same as IMS?

They're similar. Both insert fine needles into muscle to reduce tension and sensitivity. IMS follows a specific assessment and treatment approach developed for nerve-related muscle pain; your practitioner will recommend what suits you.

Are the needles safe and sterile?

Yes. We use sterile, single-use, disposable needles, and practitioners follow infection-control standards.

Is needling right for everyone?

Not always. Tell your practitioner if you're pregnant, take blood thinners, have a bleeding disorder or are nervous about needles; there are always other treatment options.

How will I feel after a needling session?

Some people feel mild soreness for a day or two, similar to after a workout. Your practitioner will tell you what to expect and how to manage it.
