FAQ

Common Questions

Does acupuncture or IMS hurt?

Most people feel little to no discomfort — sometimes a brief muscle twitch or dull ache at the point, which fades quickly. Needles are much finer than injection needles.

What's the difference between acupuncture and IMS?

Traditional acupuncture follows meridian-based points; IMS is a Western medical approach targeting specific tight muscle bands and trigger points related to nerve irritation.

Is it covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans include acupuncture coverage. We'll confirm your coverage and offer direct billing where available.

How many sessions will I need?

Some people feel relief after one session; chronic issues typically benefit from a short series. Your practitioner will discuss a plan after your first visit.
