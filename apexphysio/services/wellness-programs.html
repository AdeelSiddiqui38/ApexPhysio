HOW IT WORKS

What to Expect

A maintenance rhythm, not a one-time fix.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to be injured to join a wellness program?

Not at all — wellness programs are designed for people who want to stay ahead of pain and maintain good movement, injury or not.

How often are visits?

It depends on your goals — some people come monthly for maintenance, others seasonally. We'll suggest a rhythm based on your screen results.

Is this covered by insurance?

Many wellness visits fall under general physiotherapy, massage or kinesiology coverage — we'll help you understand what applies to your plan.

Can this be combined with other services?

Yes — wellness programs often blend elements of physiotherapy, massage and kinesiology into one ongoing plan.
