ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Car Accident (MVA) Rehab in NW Calgary

After a car accident, symptoms like neck stiffness, headaches or back pain can appear right away or days later. Being assessed early helps you understand your injury and start a plan. At Apex Physio & Wellness Clinic, physiotherapy, chiropractic care, massage and active rehab are coordinated under one roof.

Alberta's auto insurance system changes on January 1, 2027, when the government's Care-First model begins. The rules for your claim depend on the date of your collision. When you contact us, we'll help you work through the process with your insurer and, where possible, bill them directly.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to pay upfront after an accident?

Often not. Wherever possible we bill your auto insurer directly for covered treatment. What's covered depends on your policy and your collision date, so we'll confirm before you start.

How soon after an accident should I be seen?

As soon as you can after the collision, even if you feel mostly fine. Early assessment helps you understand your injury and start the claim process.

Do I need a claim number to book?

It helps but isn't strictly required for your first visit — we can guide you through what's needed for your specific insurer.

What if my symptoms started days after the accident?

That's common — some injuries, like whiplash or concussion symptoms, show up 24–72 hours later. Get assessed as soon as you notice anything.

What should I bring to my first MVA appointment?

Bring your auto insurance policy and claim number if you have one, the collision date, and any medical notes. If you don't have everything yet, contact us anyway and we'll explain the next steps.

Does Alberta's 2027 auto insurance change affect my treatment?

Collisions on or after January 1, 2027 fall under the new Care-First system; earlier collisions follow the rules in place at the time. We'll help you understand what applies to your claim.

Can I see more than one type of practitioner?

Yes. MVA plans often combine physiotherapy, chiropractic, massage and active rehab, and coordinated care at one clinic keeps everyone on the same plan.

Should I see a doctor first?

For serious symptoms such as severe headache, confusion, numbness or weakness, see a doctor or go to emergency right away. For other injuries you can book directly with us.
